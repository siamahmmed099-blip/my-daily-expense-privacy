1. Introduction

My Daily Expense is a personal expense management application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on your use of the app, the following information may be handled:

  • Expense amount, category, note and date.
  • Monthly budget amount and corresponding month.
  • Google account email address and Firebase user identifier when you sign in.

The app does not directly ask you to provide your Google password.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to manage expenses, calculate spending, prepare reports, manage monthly budgets, provide Google sign-in and support cloud backup and restore.

4. Local Storage and Cloud Backup

Expense and budget information is stored locally on your device.

Google sign-in is optional. You can use the app's general features without signing in. Cloud backup and restore require a signed-in account.

When you use cloud synchronization, relevant expense and budget information is stored using Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. The app may synchronize information in the background when an internet connection is available.

Depending on your Android device and backup settings, some app data may also be included in device-level backup services.

5. Data Sharing

Google Firebase services are used to provide account authentication and cloud storage. Information needed for these services is processed by Google under its applicable terms and privacy policies.

The developer does not sell users' personal information.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You can use the app without creating or signing into an account. If you have an account, you can use the in-app account deletion feature.

The account deletion process is designed to delete your cloud expense records, monthly budget records, local expense and budget database, and Firebase Authentication account.

Device-level backups or service-provider retention processes may be subject to their own policies and retention periods.

You can also request account deletion by emailing: siamahmmed099@gmail.com

7. Data Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect information handled by the app. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
